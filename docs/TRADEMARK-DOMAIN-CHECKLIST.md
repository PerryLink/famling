> "No exact found" = public mirrors (Aliyun/路标网/QCC) did not show an exact live mark in this pass. It is **not** a clearance opinion. A paid CNIPA/agent search is required.

### Known blockers / watch items

- **留屿**: 32 class 90777237 pending (non-core); monitor.
- **留屿园**: "园" is common in 43/44 (民宿/陵园); 9/41/42 exact looks clean, but 43/44 needs a check.
- **玩伴岛**: "玩伴" 42 class 34709744 is a cited risk; use as descriptor, not the legal mark.
- **Famling**: FANLING prior marks in 9/41; `famling.com` is registered and listed on HugeDomains; legal review needed before commercial use.
- **DeepSeek**: "DeepSeek 鲸鱼娘" is an unofficial descriptor; no official logo/wordmark; commercial product should use **Pearl / 深海鲸娘**.

## 6. What not to do

- Do not call public search "clearance."
- Do not use the official DeepSeek logo/wordmark.
- Do not file only one class (9) and assume 41/42 are safe.
- Do not use "玩伴岛" as the legal mark while "玩伴" 42 is registered.
- Do not buy `famling.com` at a four-figure price before the product has revenue.
- Do not ship commercial art while the entity/license is unresolved.
